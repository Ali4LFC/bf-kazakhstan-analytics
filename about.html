Project Overview

What this website is and what it is not.

This website analyzes the Business Funding Data in Kazakhstan dataset from Kaggle using a premium multi-page static dashboard. The goal is to present shipment patterns, value distribution, logistics structure, and dataset limitations in a polished analytical format.

Important limitation: the CSV title mentions Kazakhstan, but the actual preserved records do not provide verified Kazakhstan-specific funding data. The visible rows mainly reflect broader Asian trade activity and Bangladesh-related shipment entries.

Key Findings

Observed directly from the real preserved rows.

  • The dataset spans 13 export countries and 12 origin countries.
  • The Netherlands and India are the most frequent export partners with 3 shipments each.
  • Air transport appears most often with 9 shipments, followed closely by sea with 8.
  • The largest shipment is the Belgium-linked infant formula row worth $353,876.72.
  • Total declared value across the 20 rows is $529,789.75 USD.

Limitations

Interpret the dashboard carefully.

  • The visible sample contains only 20 shipment rows, so it is a compact analytical subset rather than a broad market census.
  • The dataset name references Kazakhstan, but the actual rows do not verify Kazakhstan-specific funding activity.
  • Several financial aggregates are carried from the original analysis and not every source metric is visible at row level in the explorer.
  • This project focuses on presentation quality and analytical honesty within a static website architecture.
